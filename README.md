Key Insights:

-Electronics drives most of the revenue (81.3%) vs. Furniture (18.7%) 

-Phone has the highest revenue but one of the lowest margins (27.8%), while Chair has the lowest revenue but the highest margin (45%)

-West generates the most revenue, but South has the highest profit margin (33.3%) — West's volume doesn't translate to the best 
profitability per sale
